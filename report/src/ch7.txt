@@SEC 7.1 Results Obtained to Date

The evaluation grid holds 3,232 rows in output/runs.csv. The main grid contributes 1,792 (32 images, four tamper classes, seven nominal tamper ratios from 0.10 to 0.70, two descriptor variants, A and C, at B = 8), the null condition 320 (32 images, two variants, five keys), and the remaining 1,120 are ablations covering B = 4 and Variant B. Over the 32-image corpus Variant A reaches a mean PSNR of 45.479 dB and a mean SSIM [21] of 0.98847, and Variant C 45.492 dB and 0.98841. The minimum SSIM over the whole B = 8 set is 0.97831, so no image is visibly degraded. The weakest named image is Tiffany at 44.07 dB for Variant A, against 45.64 to 45.73 dB for the other named images (Table 7.1).

AuSR1 publishes 45.57 dB [13]; the measured 45.479 dB for Variant A is within 0.1 dB of it. LSB shifting, which moves a descriptor-carrying pixel to the nearest value whose low bits already match, closes most of the gap to plain replacement, cutting mean distortion from about 1.5 LSB to about 0.5 LSB.

@@SUB 7.1.2 Localization

Block-level recall is 1.0000 for copy-paste splicing, crop-and-refill and noise corruption, and 0.99998 for object removal by inpainting. Across the 1,792 main-grid trials, 17 blocks were missed because the inpainting preserved their most-significant bit-planes, a predicted residual of the design. Pixel-level precision is 0.9599 as a main-grid mean, rising with tamper ratio: 0.9248, 0.9481, 0.9581, 0.9643, 0.9701, 0.9740 and 0.9797 at ratios 0.10 through 0.70. The shortfall from 1.0 is the 8x8 block-quantization penalty. Neighborhood refinement flagged only 2 blocks out of 10,092,544 verified. Block-level precision is deliberately not headlined: a block counts as tampered if the region covers any of its pixels, so a block-level false positive is structurally impossible and the metric reads 1.000 regardless of detector quality, so pixel-level precision is the honest measure.

The null condition tests unmodified watermarked images: across 320 rows, 1,802,240 independent block verifications produced 0 false-positive blocks, a rate of 0.000000 with a rule-of-three 95% upper bound of 1.66e-6. This is a determinism and consistency check across images, variants and keys, not a measurement of the 2^-32 cryptographic false-accept probability, which no experiment of this size could resolve (Table 7.3).

@@SEC 7.2 Conclusions

This project built a deterministic, training-free self-embedding fragile watermarking scheme in which each 8x8 block carries, in its two least-significant bit-planes, a 32-bit keyed HMAC-SHA256 tag and a 96-bit descriptor of a distant partner block chosen by a key-seeded single-cycle permutation. The implementation is 9,929 lines of Python across 24 modules, plus a local web application and demonstration interface, with no machine-learning framework.

Across 3,232 rows and 32 images, imperceptibility is 45.479 dB for Variant A, block-level recall is 1.0000 for three of four tamper classes, there is no false-positive block in 1,802,240 null verifications, and recoverability is 3 to 6 percentage points above its structural bound. In-region recovery, at 29.81 dB, is modest, and whole-image figures trail fountain-coded and reference-sharing schemes.

The claim is narrower than accuracy: the design offers accountability, since each verification decision is one keyed hash comparison any key holder can reproduce bit for bit. The distant-mapping idea is not claimed as new, nor is superiority over learned proactive watermarking. The scheme is fully fragile, protecting images only inside a lossless custody chain; its one-to-one backup allocation fails all-or-nothing per block, capping whole-image reconstruction at high tamper ratios; and security rests on key secrecy. Section 7.3 addresses these limits.

@@SEC 7.3 Future Work

Five directions follow from the measurements and Section 6.2.

* Reference sharing or fountain-coded descriptor allocation is the highest-value upgrade, since mapping coverage and not descriptor fidelity limits recovery quality (Section 7.1.3).
* A semi-fragile mode tolerant of benign JPEG re-encoding would relax the lossless-custody restriction, the largest deployment limit.
* Finer or hierarchical localization grids that avoid the full tag cost would raise pixel-level precision beyond 0.9599 without the penalties of B = 4.
* Integration into capture firmware and PACS or DICOM ingest would test the applications of Section 6.3 and measure embedding cost on constrained hardware.
* A formal security proof would replace the present argument from the standard properties of HMAC-SHA256 [20] with a stated reduction covering every bound field.
